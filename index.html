How to test on the phone
  1. Press SENSOR. If the phone asks for motion access, allow it.
  2. Hold the phone upright in front of you, screen toward you, like a window.
  3. Press RESET to 0. Now turn your whole body slowly to the right: the direction should say CLOCKWISE, the angle and the accumulated rotation should go up.
  4. Keep turning until you are back where you started: accumulated should be about +360° and a full turn is logged.
  5. Turn the other way, past zero: the accumulated rotation goes negative.
  6. Hold still for 20 seconds: does the accumulated number drift? Write the drift down.
  7. Tilt the phone (look up, look down, hold it flatter) while turning: does the number stay right?
